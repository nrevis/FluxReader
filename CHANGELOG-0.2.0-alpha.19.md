# Flux Reader 0.2.0-alpha.19

Visual polish pass focused on clarity, density, and consistent Material 3 hierarchy.

- Library top bar is now quieter: `Библиотека` + book count, with version/theme cycling removed from primary chrome.
- Search uses a short placeholder and one Tune button; sort/group controls stay collapsed until requested.
- Book cards drop the dedicated edit icon; primary quick actions are Speed Reader and overflow.
- Add/import, book/folder actions, and destination pickers use compact icon rows instead of heavy outlined action buttons.
- Shortened common action labels (`Изменить`, `Копировать`, `Дубликат`, `Экспорт`, `Удалить`).
- Folder cards are slightly tighter and the duplicated grouped-library count was removed.
- Reader top bar keeps TOC/Search/reading controls visible and moves fullscreen, Speed Reader, and link-return into overflow.
- Reader bottom status is shortened to `chapter/total • page/total`.
- Reader preference labels were reduced without changing their stored settings or behavior.
- Speed Reader top bar is simplified; pacing mode remains in the Speed Reader settings sheet.
- Speed Reader settings headings and live word-count label were shortened.
- App-wide Material 3 shapes and title/label weights are unified through `FluxTheme`.
- PDF page indicator formatting was tightened.
- Reader pre-pagination and alpha.18 library-management behavior remain unchanged.
