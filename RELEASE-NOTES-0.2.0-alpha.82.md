# Flux Reader 0.2.0-alpha.82

- Unified, compact Reader chrome: Back / chapter title / overflow menu. Search, bookmarks, typography, narration, chapters, effects and link history move to ⋮; no fullscreen or audio icon in toolbar. Center tap still toggles immersive mode.
- Speed Reader settings: dedicated Voice and Narration pane with offline Android TTS voices, rate, pitch, and auto-chapter preference.
- Experimental Wasteland/Cyberpunk screen effects are optional and persistent: master CRT, scanlines, glow, static noise, vignette, strength 0–100%. Accessible from Appearance and reading screens.
- Existing books, bookmark positions, and settings are preserved.
- Static critics for the above structural regressions. Android compile must be completed on the user's Mac.
